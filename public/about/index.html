About

Hello! 👋 and welcome.

I’m a full-stack software engineer with over a decade of experience building robust systems across enterprise software, FinTech, and aerospace industries. I specialize in designing internal tools and developer platforms that accelerate product delivery and empower engineering teams to ship high-quality code. I especially enjoy tackling complex architecture challenges and turning developer friction into streamlined, scalable workflows.

I’m a builder at heart, even in my downtime. Lately my personal technical curiosity has been focused on exploring the depths of systems programming and the elegance of functional programming (I’m a huge fan of the C and Lisp-family of programming languages). When it’s time to unplug, I head outdoors for a trail run or unwind with an immersive science fiction or fantasy book.
